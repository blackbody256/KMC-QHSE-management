| DEC-032 | What does the year-to-date value mean for each KPI? | `KMC_HWMS_SRS_v2.1_DRAFT.md:130` calls every year-to-date cell a monthly average. | DEC-033 | Which month receives referral sick leave that spans a month end? | `services/clinical/referral.go`, `LeavePeriod`, assigns the whole contribution to the month of `sickLeaveFrom`, with feedback date and referral date as fallbacks. | DEC-034 | How must the clinical service authenticate when it delivers leave facts to metrics? | `services/clinical/publisher.go` sends a bearer shared secret to the internal metrics route, and `services/metrics/handlers.go` compares it directly. `deploy/keycloak/realm-hwms.json` registers no clinical-to-metrics client.
